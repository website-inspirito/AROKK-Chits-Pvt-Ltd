A chit is a group savings and borrowing scheme. Every member pays a fixed amount each month, and each month one member takes the full amount through an auction.

Registered chit companies operate under the Chit Funds Act, 1982. The company must deposit security with the Registrar for every chit group it runs.

The discount given up by the auction winner, minus the foreman's commission (capped at 7% by law), is shared equally among all members.

Visit your nearest branch or call us. You can join a suitable group with basic KYC documents.

ID proof (Aadhaar or Voter ID), address proof and one passport-size photo.

Chit funds: disciplined savings, money when you need it

In a chit group, every member pays a fixed amount each month. Each month one member takes the full chit through an auction, and the discount they give up is shared with everyone as a dividend.

From joining to getting your money

Step 1

Choose a scheme

Pick a chit value and duration that match what you can save each month.

Step 2

Complete KYC and join

Sign the chit agreement with your ID and address proof.

Step 3

Pay every month

Pay your subscription at the branch or through the modes AROKK offers.

Step 4

Monthly auction

Members who need money bid. The highest discount offered wins that month's chit.

Step 5

Dividend shared

The discount minus commission is split equally, so everyone pays less next month.

Step 6

Receive your prize money

Once your surety documents are verified, the prize amount is paid to you.

Chit terms, made simple

Chit value

The total amount of the group, for example ₹1,00,000.

Subscription

The amount each member pays every month.

Auction and bid

The discount a member is willing to give up to take the chit that month.

Foreman commission

The company's fee for running the chit, capped by law at 7% of the chit value.

Dividend

The discount minus commission, shared equally by all members.

Prize amount

What the winner receives: chit value minus the discount.

Surety

A guarantee for your remaining instalments, such as a guarantor or documents.

Security deposit

What the company deposits with the Registrar for every group.
